Reçel Games Steam Demo Steam Next Fest

When the soundtrack is the combat system.

Beatpunk is a bullet-heaven shooter built around a looping synthwave track. I built a procedural music system in FMOD that assembles the soundtrack in real time from short instrument samples as players unlock abilities: every ability adds a layer, and the track evolves with how the player fights.

About

Trained in music. Working in games.

Hi, I'm Kıvanç, a sound designer and composer for games based in Toronto. I hold a Master's in Sonic Arts from Istanbul Technical University and a Bachelor's in Jazz Guitar, ranked 1st in my program. I've been the full-time audio department for two game studios and now take on freelance and contract work, most recently for Meta through TEKSystems.
